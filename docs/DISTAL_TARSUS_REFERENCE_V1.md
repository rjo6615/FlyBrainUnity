Prospective scope, before inspecting newly acquired metadata rows:
four currently unmapped middle/hind Tarsus1 motor coordinates. Current admission
remains 20/42. This is a reference-data investigation, not a motor assay or
permission to enable channels.

Question: can published BANC materialization888 annotations provide a traceable
reference lead for the missing middle/hind tarsus levator/depressor populations
in the existing MaleCNS v1.0 runtime?

Use the official publication source and schema, preserving the exact raw source,
revision/generation, checksum, attribution and all selected original fields.
Search all tarsus levator/depressor records, including front-leg controls; retain
missing and ambiguous matches. Do not select by activity, connectivity strength,
outcome or convenient population size. Keep long-tendon/claw annotations separate.

The published BANC schema describes MaleCNS v0.9 cell-type labels/confidence and
top NBLAST candidates; it does not promise exact MaleCNS v1.0 body IDs. Therefore:

- Never treat BANC root IDs as MaleCNS IDs, round IDs through floating point,
  transfer laterality, or mirror front-leg pools to another leg.
- A top NBLAST candidate or shared class/name alone is a research lead, not an
  independently resolved per-neuron function.
- Any proposed exact runtime identity must resolve the dataset/version bridge,
  complete population membership, side/segment/nerve, original annotations and
  unique body-ID/dense-index correspondence. Preserve every ambiguity or mismatch.
- Keep author-curated annotations separate from pipeline-derived labels. Trace
  derivation and match confidence before claiming new anatomical support.
- No production maps, admission flags, signs, gains, drive, thresholds or seeds
  change. No CNS/physics initialization or motion experiment belongs to this scope.
