# Coxa motor hypothesis track V1 — design

Status: **design only**. No code, no run, and no change to the 20 admitted channels.
Every number below is fixed before any D1/D2/D3 outcome is inspected. Step protocols
will be hash-locked in code and committed before each runs once.

## Question

Hair-plate Step C showed that SNpp45 hair plates make coxa motor neurons (MNs)
posture-sensitive and recruit the posterior-moving pools, as the published CxHP8
resistance reflex predicts. The coxa joints that those MNs should move (18
coordinates) are not admitted, so the loop is open on the motor side.

This track asks: **if coxa MN pools are bound to body joints by a rule fixed in
advance from muscle names and measured body geometry, does the closed loop
(hair plate -> CNS -> coxa joint -> hair plate) behave as the published resistance
reflex predicts, and does it do so better than a sign-flipped control and the
competing bodymap-label binding?**

## What this is and is not

| Is | Is not |
|---|---|
| A labelled **hypothesis** binding (`COXA_HYPOTHESIS_H1/H2`) | An admission. Motor coverage stays **20/42 proven**. |
| A falsifiable prediction from independent biology (resistance reflex) | Proof of the mapping. Consistency is the only possible pass. |
| Reuse of the existing supplemental-channel path, decoder and bounds | New gains, thresholds, weights, drive or seeds |
| Assisted standing (posture servos on) | Balance, posture rescue or gait |

Known engineering limit, carried from the Stage 2 review: in the live-v3 kernel a
neural joint adds an offset to its posture-holding servo target. D3 therefore
measures the **direction and size of a setpoint shift and its sensory
consequence**, not muscle force or free posture.

## Facts this design rests on (already in the repository)

- **Bodymap pools** (`fly-brain-main/public/data/bodymap.json`, per leg):
  `coxa` label: T1 promotor (4) + remotor/abductor (2); T2/T3 remotor/abductor
  only. `coxa_abduct`: remotor/abductor + sternal adductor (1). `coxa_twist`:
  sternal anterior rotator (2) + sternal posterior rotator (4). Remotor/abductor
  body IDs appear under two labels. That is the audit's `SHARED_AXIS_BINDING_REQUIRED`
  blocker, and the supplemental resolver correctly refuses a body ID in two channels.
- **Audit V1.1:** the 18 coxa rows have exact candidate identities but no binding
  or sign. Coxa_yaw signs were `ANATOMICAL_SIGN_GEOMETRICALLY_UNRESOLVED`.
- **Step B:** `+joint_<leg>Coxa` moves the coxa posteriorly in all legs.
- **Kernel limits:** coxa joints are unlimited and actuators have ctrlrange ±1e6,
  so the existing `safe_contribution_bound` yields the decoder maximum
  `DECODER_MAX_RAD = 0.25 rad`. The existing activation (`HALF_ACTIVATION_HZ = 17`),
  observer (40 ms), slew (4 rad/s) and decoder modes are reused unchanged.

## Step D1 — coxa action table (geometry only, no CNS)

On the FlyGym body built by the existing runtime, at the Step B/B4 standing pose,
for each leg and each of its three coxa DoFs (Coxa, Coxa_roll, Coxa_yaw):

1. **Joint action vector:** displacement of the leg tip (tarsus end) in the Thorax
   frame per +0.01 rad, by central finite difference. Each other joint is held.
   Components: anterior (+x), outward (lateral, side-signed), dorsal.
2. **Walking range:** peak-to-peak angle of the DoF in the FlyGym recorded
   untethered walking step (`single_steps_untethered.pkl`, sha 1e5b28…, the Step B
   source). This is independent of names and of the connectome.

**Named actions** (unit vectors, fixed here; outward = away from the midline):

| Pool name | Named action |
|---|---|
| Tergopleural/Pleural promotor | anterior |
| Pleural remotor/abductor | (posterior + outward)/√2 |
| Sternal adductor | inward |
| Sternal anterior rotator | anterior |
| Sternal posterior rotator | posterior |

The rotator entry uses the swing reading used by the CxHP8 paper for T2/T3
(posterior rotator = posterior-moving). A long-axis twist reading is the
competing hypothesis H2 below and is not discarded.

**D1 checks (geometry, preregistered):**
- D1a: all 18 action vectors are finite and nonzero (> 1e-6 mm per 0.01 rad).
- D1b: `+joint_<leg>Coxa` anterior component < 0 in all six legs (reproduces Step B).
- D1c (independent cross-check, reported and labelled, not a gate): in each
  leg, is the DoF that H1 assigns the swing pools (promotor/remotor/rotators)
  also the DoF with the largest recorded walking range? Six yes/no answers.

## Step D2 — two hypothesis bindings, generated by rule

**Assignment rule** (shared): for each pool in a leg, compute the cosine between
its named action and each candidate joint's action vector. Assign the pool to the
candidate with the largest |cos|. The sign is `sign(cos)`: positive pools move the
joint +, negative pools move it −. If the largest |cos| is < 0.5, the pool is
**unassigned** and reported. Each pool goes to exactly one joint, which resolves
the shared remotor/abductor IDs without a last-record-wins choice.

- **H1 (name-geometry):** candidates are all three coxa DoFs of that leg.
- **H2 (bodymap-label):** candidates are restricted to the joints the bodymap
  labels for that pool (`coxa`->Coxa, `coxa_abduct`->Coxa_roll,
  `coxa_twist`->Coxa_yaw, as in the audit). The rotators are therefore forced onto
  Coxa_yaw (twist reading).

**Channel construction:** one supplemental channel per joint that receives pools.
If both signs are present, use `ANTAGONIST_PAIR` (coordinate_sign +1); if one,
use `UNIDIRECTIONAL_POSITIVE` with that sign. Joints receiving no pool keep their
baseline servo, as today. **H1_FLIP** is H1 with every coordinate sign negated.
If H1 and H2 produce identical channel sets, H2 is not run, and that is recorded.

The D2 output (tables, body IDs, dense indices, cosines) is committed before D3.

## Step D3 — closed-loop resistance-reflex test (CNS + physics)

Setup is identical to Step C arm **A_ON** (assisted posture, hair plates ON, same
seed, schedule, encoder and analysis window 520–2000 ms), plus the supplemental
coxa channels.

| Arm | Coxa channels |
|---|---|
| C0 | none (re-run of Step C A_ON) |
| H1 | H1 binding |
| H1_FLIP | H1, all coxa signs negated |
| H2 | H2 binding (skipped if identical to H1) |

**Integrity (all must pass, else `INCONCLUSIVE_INTEGRITY`, with no labels):**
- I1: every arm completes; no instability flag; no unauthorized writes.
- I2: identical external-drive uniforms hashes in all arms.
- I3: C0 neural spike record equals the saved Step C A_ON record (reproducibility).
- I4: the 20 historical channels have identical definitions in all arms. Every
  coxa contribution satisfies |c| ≤ 0.25 rad.
- I5: arms are identical until the first nonzero coxa contribution.

**Primary measure:** per firing leg, the window-mean change, relative to C0, in the
coxa forward component (the Step B encoder geometry; continuous, not saturated).
Firing legs are those whose C0 hair-plate rate is > 0 in the window (from Step C:
LF, RF, LH, RH). Middle legs are reported but excluded because their encoder is
silent.

**Q3 — resistance-reflex consistency (labels fixed now):**
- `RESISTANCE_CONSISTENT` if, in at least 3 of the firing legs, H1 shifts the
  forward component **posteriorly** by ≥ 0.02 (unit-vector component, about 1°),
  and H1_FLIP shifts it anteriorly by ≥ 0.02 in at least 3 of the same legs.
- `SIGN_ONLY_CONSISTENT` if the H1 condition holds but the FLIP condition does
  not.
- otherwise `NOT_CONSISTENT`.

**Q4 — H1 vs H2:** the same posterior-shift count for H2. Report
`H1_FAVOURED`, `H2_FAVOURED` or `NOT_DISTINGUISHED` (equal counts). This is a
consistency ranking, not identification.

**Secondary, reported but not gated:** per-leg hair-plate delivered rate (expected
to fall with H1 and rise with FLIP, but limited by encoder saturation at rest);
coxa MN rates; per-channel saturation and slew time; body tilt; coxa contribution
time courses.

## Interpretation limits (stated before running)

- One seed, assisted posture, and an offset-on-servo actuation model.
- Step C already shows hair plates excite posterior pools, so a pass of H1 is
  partly expected. The informative outcomes are a **failure** (the whole chain
  does not produce resistance in the live body), the FLIP contrast, network or
  cross-leg effects, and H1 vs H2.
- A pass is consistency with one published reflex; it does not validate the
  binding, admit coordinates, or show balance. Middle legs are untested by this
  sense. The 0.02 effect bar is about 1°, chosen as a clear-above-noise geometric
  change, and is not tied to any expected outcome.

## Roadmap after this track

If D3 is `RESISTANCE_CONSISTENT`, the next separately preregistered question is
the released-servo (R) posture test with hypothesis channels, compared against
V1.2 R_NC and Step C R_ON. If it fails, the failure is recorded, and alternatives
(H2, encoder version, more seeds) become separate versioned questions.

Expected cost: D1/D2 take seconds. D3 is 3–4 arms of about 5 minutes each.
